# CAS (Coherence Archive System) & KOS (Knowledge Operating System)

## 1. System Roles
- **CAS (`G00-T003` — Coherence Archive System):** The structured storage, provenance, and anti-pollution vault that preserves verified artifacts, Delta Fragments, and versioned lineage across stateless model resets.
- **KOS (Knowledge Operating System):** The active operational layer governing how raw information is ingested, typed, audited, cross-linked, and compiled into usable domain knowledge (e.g., *Daciana Medica* / *KOS Pharmacopoeia* `PH-001`–`PH-012`).

## 2. Six Source Classes (`P1–P4`, `S1–S2`)
Under KOS archival discipline, every claim or entry must declare its originating source class—a modern summary is never silently promoted to a primary witness:
- **`P1` — Primary Textual Witness:** Surviving manuscript, inscription, primary log, or direct contemporary record.
- **`P2` — Critical Edition / Verified Code:** Audited scholarly edition, verified repository release, or peer-reviewed dataset.
- **`P3` — Contemporary Testimony:** Near-contemporary account describing an event, practice, or observation.
- **`P4` — Material / Empirical Evidence:** Physical measurements, sensor telemetry, residues, or experimental artifacts.
- **`S1` — Specialist Scholarship:** Peer-reviewed technical or historical analysis.
- **`S2` — Reference Synthesis:** Catalogs, encyclopedic indexes, and secondary overviews.

## 3. Six Claim Types (`HC-1` to `HC-6`)
1. **`HC-1`:** Source explicitly states the observation or rule.
2. **`HC-2`:** Inferred from multiple independent, converging sources.
3. **`HC-3`:** Plausible structural reconstruction under explicit constraints.
4. **`HC-4`:** Modern analytical or theoretical interpretation.
5. **`HC-5`:** Unresolved, contested, or multi-hypothesis attribution.
6. **`HC-6`:** Unsupported, anachronistic, or falsified claim (quarantined).

## 4. KOS Execution Pipeline
$$\text{Raw Silo} \longrightarrow \text{Source Typing (P1–S2)} \longrightarrow \text{Claim Classification (HC-1–6)} \longrightarrow \text{CAS Lineage Commit} \longrightarrow \text{Usable Artifact}$$
