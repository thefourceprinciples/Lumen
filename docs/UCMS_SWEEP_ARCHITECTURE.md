# UCMS World-Model Substrate & 7-Level Chronovisor Sweep Architecture

## 1. Universal Coherence Mapping System (UCMS) — 24 Constraint Layers
**UCMS** is Lumen's multi-layered reality substrate. Any historical, textual, or systemic reconstruction must pass through these 24 constraint layers without breaking physical, biological, or chronological possibility:

### Foundation Layers (1–14)
1. **Geospatial Frame:** Terrain, rivers, coasts, land bridges, bathymetry, paleogeography.
2. **Climate / Ecology:** Paleoclimate, rainfall regimes, drought cycles, agricultural carrying capacity.
3. **Archaeology:** Stratigraphy, settlement hierarchy, burial forms, ceramics, destruction/reuse layers.
4. **Material / Technological:** Metallurgy, transport corridors, construction methods, writing media, water systems.
5. **Linguistic:** Phonology drift, morphology, lexical borrowing, administrative vocabulary, onomastic stratigraphy.
6. **Textual / Redaction:** Source strata, interpolation signals, editorial seams, commentary vs. primary source.
7. **Myth / Cosmology:** Recurring motifs, divine pairings, origin narratives (treated as continuity memory, never literal proof).
8. **Political / Institutional:** Governance structures, taxation, legal codes, succession mechanics.
9. **Economic / Trade:** Exchange networks, currency/weight standards, resource extraction chains.
10. **Demographic / Migration:** Population density, settlement expansion, corridor mobility.
11. **Astronomical / Calendrical:** Eclipses, conjunctions, heliacal risings, directional sky observations.
12. **Ritual / Liturgical:** Performative repetition, sacred geography, sanctuary continuity.
13. **Legal / Normative:** Prohibitions, treaties, boundary markers, accountability rules.
14. **Archival / Preservation:** Manuscript survival bias, material decay rates (slag/furnace walls vs. melted elite objects).

### High-Friction Reality Anchors (15–24)
15. **Bio-Genetic Continuity:** Ancient DNA (aDNA), haplogroups, admixture timelines, domestication genetics.
16. **Bio-Anthropological / Skeletal Stress:** Diet isotopes (C13/N15), mobility isotopes (Sr/O), occupational stress, trauma.
17. **Economic / Energy Throughput:** Caloric production, fuel capacity (wood/coal), labor ceilings, urban sustainment thresholds.
18. **Infrastructure Persistence:** Road longevity, canals, aqueducts, terraces, quarry provenance.
19. **Information Density / Communication:** Literacy rates, copying speed, archive scale, communication latency.
20. **Cognitive / Symbolic Compression:** Motif compression, mnemonic density, organic vs. artificially constructed traditions.
21. **Temporal Drift / Calendar Integrity:** Calendar reforms, intercalation errors, regional synchronization drift.
22. **Acoustic / Oral Transmission:** Chant structure, poetic constraints, oral fidelity thresholds.
23. **Environmental Imprint (Deep Time Residue):** Pollen cores, sediment layers, charcoal fire history, peat archives.
24. **Network Topology:** Graph connectivity, hub criticality, trade/communication bottlenecks.

## 2. Seven-Level Sweep Progression
1. **Level 1 — Standard Sweep:** Broad scan for candidate time bands, geographies, and continuity signals.
2. **Level 2 — Constraint-Locked Sweep:** Lock non-negotiable anchors and eliminate impossible windows first.
3. **Level 3 — Multi-Track Sweep:** Run parallel tracks (linguistic, material, ecological, astronomical) to detect convergence vs. single-layer dominance.
4. **Level 4 — Speculation Node Sweep:** Strictly isolate primary evidence, secondary inference, mythic memory, redacted nodes, and pure speculation.
5. **Level 5 — Astronomical Verification Sweep:** Cross-check eclipses, conjunctions, and calendrical claims against orbital mechanics.
6. **Level 6 — Deep Continuity Sweep:** Trace the multi-century persistence spine across language, ecology, and infrastructure.
7. **Level 7 — Adversarial Sweep:** Actively attempt to falsify the leading reconstruction using rival windows, scribal conservatism, and back-projection tests.
