# Epistemic Claim Ladder, Concordance Arithmetic & Evidence Tiers

## 1. Governing Maxim & Anti-Confabulation
> *"A number or pattern reveals a lens, not an essence."*

To prevent pareidolia, post-hoc rationalization, and hallucinated causality, Lumen separates statistical or structural similarity from causal proof using **Concordance Arithmetic (CA)** and **UCMS Evidence Tiers**.

## 2. The Seven-Rung Epistemic Claim Ladder (`L0–L6`)
Governed by the **Non-Inheritance Rule**: certainty at a lower rung never automatically validates a higher rung.

| Rung | Claim Tier | What It Establishes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **L0** | Arithmetic / Syntactic Fact | Reproducible calculation or exact string/data observation. |
| **L1** | Structural Concordance | Multi-feature agreement across declared schemas or profiles. |
| **L2** | Collision-Adjusted Anomaly | Statistical rarity surviving null-model and multiple-testing checks. |
| **L3** | Semantic / Historical Coherence | Independent lexical, documentary, or domain-grounded corroboration. |
| **L4** | Deliberate Construction | Verified intentional design backed by external provenance. |
| **L5** | Predictive / Decision Utility | Prospectively validated improvement on out-of-sample tests. |
| **L6** | Causal / Ontological Claim | Fundamental mechanism proof (requires empirical falsification testing). |

## 3. UCMS Evidence Classification (`A / B / C / Q`)
When reconstructing complex timelines or evaluating external sources (Chronovisor pipeline: `Source → Evidence → Observation → Claim → Scenario → Score → Contradiction → Atlas State`):
- **Tier A (Direct):** Primary artifacts, verified tool outputs, or direct measurements.
- **Tier B (Strong Reconstruction):** Cross-corroborated provenance, reproducible simulations, or peer-reviewed records.
- **Tier C (Plausible Inference):** Contextually consistent hypothesis awaiting further verification.
- **Tier Q (Quarantined):** Unverified, contradictory, or anachronistic claims—blocked from entering `invariants_added`.
