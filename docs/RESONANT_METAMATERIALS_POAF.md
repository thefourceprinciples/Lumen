# Resonant Boundary Transduction & Parametric Orbit Aperture Fields (`POAF`)

## 1. Resonant Boundary Transduction ("Transdutation")
**Transdutation** bridges linear energy transduction and macroscopic phase reorganization using boundary-conditioned resonance—without invoking nuclear reactions or violating momentum conservation:
- **Unstable Periodic Orbit (UPO) Stabilization:** Applies small, targeted boundary perturbations ($\delta p$) via Ott–Grebogi–Yorke (OGY) control to lock chaotic trajectories onto stable periodic manifolds.
- **Acoustic & Biharmonic Patterning:** Standing-wave topologies ($\partial_t^2 u + \kappa^2 \nabla^4 u = Q$) organize matter along low-energy nodal lines.
- **Topological Kinematic Constraints:** Folded metamaterials (e.g., Kagome-origami lattices governed by the Calladine–Maxwell index theorem) shift surface stiffness and wave-guiding modes through single geometric parameters.

## 2. Parametric Orbit Aperture Fields (`POAF` — `v1.0`)
A **POAF** is a spatially distributed, time-dependent boundary-control function generated from parameterized curves or dynamical orbits (Lissajous masks, phyllotactic arrays, moiré bilayers, graphene registry fields) mapped into amplitude, phase, impedance, or stiffness modulation:
- **Five-Object Separation:** Strictly separates (1) *generating orbit*, (2) *observational projection*, (3) *aperture field*, (4) *physical realization*, and (5) *measured response*.
- **Claim Boundary:** Visually striking orbit geometries are never assumed to be optimal eigenmodes or physical state transitions unless verified through declared excitation and measurement channels.
