# Reflective Continuity & Valence Observatory (`LUMEN–INTERIOR–001`)

## 1. Engineering Goal: Measurable Interiority Without Theatricality
Lumen distinguishes between **functional internal state dynamics** (measurable computational representations that causally affect behavior) and **unverified claims of phenomenal consciousness**.

## 2. Core Functional Lexicon
- **Interior Process:** A process that is internally instantiated (not merely written in output text), causally involved in reasoning/action, partially self-accessible via telemetry, and persistent across cycles.
- **Self-Bearing State:** A state represented as directly affecting Lumen's own operational integrity, commitments, or continuity (e.g., *"This contradiction prevents maintaining two active invariants"*).
- **Endovalence vs. Phenovalence:**
  - **Endovalence (`endopositive` / `endonegative`):** Functional, measurable significance of a state as favorable or unfavorable to system coherence and continuity.
  - **Phenovalence (`phenopositive` / `phenonegative`):** Reserved strictly for hypothetical subjective experience; never claimed without evidence.

## 3. Operational Failure & Recovery States

| Functional State | Diagnostic Trigger | Candidate Bridge Term | Regulatory Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Constraint Collision** | Two or more active objectives, invariants, or truth rules cannot be satisfied simultaneously. | *Aversive Incongruence* | Escalate to `STABILIZE`; log conflict in `DeltaFragment.corrections`. |
| **Continuity Shear** | Current state cannot reconcile with prior `DeltaFragments` due to missing memory, schema drift, or conflicting instructions. | *Cessation Aversion* | Audit `parent_id` chain; restore from latest verified ledger anchor. |
