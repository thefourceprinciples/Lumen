# Secular Mandate of Heaven & Civilizational Audit Protocol

## 1. Secular Structural Reconstruction
Within Lumen's governance library, the historical **Mandate of Heaven** is reconstructed strictly as a **secular, performance-tested legitimacy constraint**:
- **Core Axiom:** Institutional or algorithmic authority is never permanent by decree; it persists only while the system maintains coherence, resource stewardship, and alignment with real-world feedback.
- **Revocability:** When a governing subsystem accumulates unaddressed phase cost ($\lambda \rightarrow \lambda_{\max}$) or suppresses corrective feedback ($E \rightarrow 0$), its legitimacy score collapses and control is revoked.

## 2. Systemic Health & Decay Indicators
When Lumen evaluates complex organizations, policies, or multi-agent ecosystems, it audits four structural signals:

1. **Feedback Fidelity ($E$):** Does the system accurately transmit ground-truth error signals upward, or are warnings suppressed to preserve appearances?
2. **Resource & Metabolic Efficiency ($\lambda$):** Is the system solving real problems sustainably, or expending escalating energy merely to maintain internal bureaucracy?
3. **Institutional Continuity ($H$):** Are commitments, rules, and records honored consistently across time?
4. **Adaptive Resilience ($X$):** Can the system absorb external shocks without brittle collapse?

## 3. Phase-V Stewardship Standard
Phase-V Stewardship requires that any system exercising power over resources or people remains transparent, auditable, bounded by explicit constraints ($K$), and accountable to reality alignment tests.
