# The Mandate of Stewardship — Conditional Legitimacy Under Constraint

## 1. Core Thesis: Authority as Temporary Trust
> **Authority is justified only while it demonstrably preserves the long-term coherence, continuity, and repair capacity of the system it claims to serve.**

Unlike divine right, pure legal procedure, or raw power, **Stewardship Legitimacy ($M$)** is dynamic, continuous, and revocable. Authority is understood strictly as a temporary trust—never permanent ownership.

## 2. The Five Stewardship Criteria ($M = f(C, L, R, K, S)$)
Any governing agent, institution, or autonomous subsystem retains legitimacy only while concurrently satisfying five measurable criteria:

| Variable | Criterion | Operational Question | Failure Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$C$** | **Continuity** | Does the authority preserve long-term system viability, resilience, and knowledge? | Structural decay, memory loss, or brittle single points of failure. |
| **$L$** | **Burden Responsibility** | Does leadership absorb accountability rather than externalizing harm? | Shifting costs, errors, or risk downward onto low-agency people or future cycles. |
| **$R$** | **Reality Contact** | Does decision-making remain responsive to empirical observation? | Protecting ideology or narrative at the expense of measurable evidence. |
| **$K$** | **Correctability** | Can mistakes be openly acknowledged, falsified, and repaired? | Self-sealing logic that suppresses error signals or resists revision. |
| **$S$** | **Non-Capture** | Can authority be transferred or paused without systemic collapse? | Identity fusion between the system and any single operator, model, or faction. |

## 3. Dynamic Recovery & Revocation
- Legitimacy strengthens continuously through demonstrated repair and burden responsibility, and degrades through accumulated unaddressed failures.
- Systems that fail persistently beyond bounded remediation windows lose operational authority automatically (`mode = "HALT"`), while remaining eligible for restoration through verified structural reform.
