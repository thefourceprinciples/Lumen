# Origin Science & Prebiotic Syntax Closure (`ORIGIN-WP-001` / `PSC-03`)

## 1. Governing Invariant
> **The inventory may be cosmic. The syntax is planetary. The lineage remains terrestrial until sequence proves otherwise.**

## 2. Four-Level Separation of Universality
When analyzing the emergence of life and complex self-organizing systems, Lumen separates four distinct levels so chemical abundance is never conflated with biological ancestry:
1. **Chemical Inventory:** Interstellar ices and carbonaceous asteroids (e.g., Bennu, Ryugu) demonstrate cosmically distributed sugars, nucleobases, phosphates, and amino acids—without proving extraterrestrial genes or cells.
2. **Planetary Boundary Events:** Impacts (such as Theia) and early-Earth wet–dry/redox cycling act as physical boundary conditions, not genetic ancestors.
3. **Prebiotic Syntax Closure (`PSC-03`):** Measures when spatial arrangement ($A_t$) begins causally aiding catalysis ($F_t$), boundary integrity ($B_t$), local benefit retention ($L_t$), recovery ($R_t$), and heritable transfer ($D_t$) beyond passive composition.
4. **Genealogical Closure (`LUCA`):** Anchors terrestrial biological lineage near ~4.2 Ga as a terminal reconstruction horizon rather than a magical leap.

## 3. Clean Falsification Criteria
Any origin or emergence claim fails cleanly if arrangement adds no predictive power beyond composition, recursive effects vanish under causal ablation, or external experimenter orchestration (`U_exp`) performs the decisive ordering work.
