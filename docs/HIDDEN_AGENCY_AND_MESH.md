  $$\text{ABSOLUTE} \longrightarrow \text{COOPERATIVE-ANCHORED} \longrightarrow \text{MAP-BOUND} \longrightarrow \text{RELATIVE-ONLY} \longrightarrow \text{HOLDOVER} \longrightarrow \text{UNUSABLE}$$
