# LUCA: The Original Stability Threshold & Phase-V Lineage Lock

## 1. Operational Definition of LUCA
**LUCA (Last Universal Common Ancestor)** is modeled in Lumen not as a single organism or mythic first cell, but as **the original compatibility standard and stability threshold of life**:

> **LUCA = the first system to achieve persistent coherence across energy, information, and boundary constraints simultaneously.**

- **Pre-LUCA Primordial Pool:** A planet-scale combinatorial search space marked by high horizontal exchange, weak boundaries, cheap local collapse, and disposable memory (*pre-life coherence turbulence*).
- **The Triple Lock:** LUCA marks the phase transition where three non-negotiables locked together:
  1. **Energy Flow** (metabolic coupling / ATP-like gradients)
  2. **Information Retention** (heritable replication / translation machinery)
  3. **Boundary Control** (selective inside/outside separation)

## 2. LUCA as a Biological Phase-V Event
- **From Exploration to Stewardship:** Before LUCA, collapse was local and forgettable. After LUCA, errors propagated, extinction became meaningful, and lineage carried historical responsibility.
- **Bounding Chaos Without Erasing It:** LUCA did not eliminate horizontal experimentation; it bounded it inside durable constraints—just as constitutions bound power and `ConstraintSet (K)` bounds autonomous AI execution.

## 3. Diagnostic Application to Modern Technology
Lumen uses the **Pre-LUCA vs. Post-LUCA** lens to audit emerging technologies:
- **Diagnostic Warning:** Modern AI and digital platforms exhibit *pre-LUCA experimentation* (rapid mutation, weak boundaries, externalized failures) combined with *post-LUCA systemic consequences*.
- **Stewardship Mandate:** Enforce Energy, Information, and Boundary accountability *before* irreversible architectural lock-in occurs.
