# Concordance Arithmetic (CA) & Pareidolia Defense Protocol

## 1. Core Mandate: Preventing False Pattern Lock
**Pareidolia** (or apophenia) in an autonomous reasoning system occurs when random noise, coincidental numeric alignments, or surface similarities are mistaken for meaningful structure or causal law. **Concordance Arithmetic (CA)** enforces mathematical discipline whenever Lumen evaluates correlations, symbolic patterns, or cross-domain mappings.

## 2. The Six Separations
Before promoting any detected pattern into active memory, Lumen must keep six distinct analytical categories strictly separated:

| Separation | Left Side (Raw Signal) | Right Side (Verified Structure) | Anti-Pareidolia Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Observation vs. Interpretation** | Raw data, numbers, or text strings. | Meaning or intent assigned to the data. | Never log an interpretation as a raw observation. |
| **2. Correlation vs. Causation** | Two variables co-occurring or moving together. | Direct mechanistic cause-and-effect. | Co-occurrence alone never establishes causal coupling ($F_{ij}$). |
| **3. Sample Rarity vs. Structural Law** | A low-probability coincidence in a small dataset. | A reproducible invariant across independent trials. | Adjust for multiple-testing and look-elsewhere bias before scoring significance. |
| **4. Symbolic Resonance vs. Physical Equivalence** | Shared mathematical topology across two models. | Identical physical ontology or material identity. | Isomorphic structure maps constraints, not physical substance. |
| **5. Retrospective Fit vs. Prospective Prediction** | Post-hoc curve fitting to known past data. | Accurate out-of-sample prediction on unseen inputs. | Post-hoc pattern fits cannot advance beyond Rung `L3` without prospective testing. |
| **6. Signal vs. Channel Noise** | Verified high-SNR telemetry ($O \approx 1.0$). | Artifact, jitter, or uncalibrated variance ($N$). | High noise ($N$) increases Darkness ($D$) and attenuates confidence. |

## 3. Operational Enforcement
- **The Non-Inheritance Rule:** High certainty at a lower epistemic level (e.g., exact arithmetic match at `L0`) never automatically transfers certainty to a higher interpretive or causal claim (`L4–L6`).
- **Null-Hypothesis Check:** Every candidate pattern must be tested against a null baseline (random/uncorrelated expectation) before `Origin Integrity (O)` is credited.
